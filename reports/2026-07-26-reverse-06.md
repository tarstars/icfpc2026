## Design

The double-extraction algorithm is retained. The former 2×4 relay interior
becomes a 3×3 room with a six-tick steady-state loop. Its five-row outer room
fits below the nine-row pump, allowing a 14×14 machine.

The compact relay enters the pump through its left wall. Because `U` turns
away from the selected incoming pipe, this changes the post-receive heading
from south to east. The pump therefore uses a six-cell `U m d / ^ s <`
cycle: `m` moves before `d`, and the previous pre-`U` decrement is removed.
Positive BP values relay and return; BP zero exits east to the unchanged
two-value output protocol. `B=1` is reloaded on the home climb.

The 15-cell ring is folded through the lower band without grazing the output
room. Every `r`/`s` cell has an asserted pipe index; head/relay sends select
the ring, output sends select O, and every receive selects relay-to-pump.
