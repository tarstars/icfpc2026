## Architecture

The candidate generated by `src/littleman/tcp.py` uses one controller and a
relay joined by a pipe ring. Buffered packets occupy two consecutive ring
values: absolute sequence number followed by packet value.

The controller keeps the next expected sequence number in `B`. For each input
packet it:

1. computes `seq - expected`;
2. copies the delay to `BP` and decrements it 15 times, so a positive result
   detects the required `delay >= 16` loss condition;
3. enqueues `(seq, val)`;
4. waits for all ring values to park, then uses `q` and `]` to count pairs;
5. scans for `seq == expected`, requeueing nonmatches;
6. removes a match, increments `expected`, emits its value, and scans again.

The measured incoming ring pipe has capacity 38, above the worst-case 32
values for 16 buffered packet pairs. Both paths that precede `q` are longer
than a complete ring round trip.
