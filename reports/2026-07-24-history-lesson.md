## Encoding

Each ASCII byte `c` becomes a nonzero radix-92 digit `c - 31`. An offline
dynamic program partitions the digits into signed-64-bit-safe packed words
while jointly minimizing data rows and decimal-literal source cells.

The accepted layout minimizes the word count subject to an 18-decimal-digit
limit. Its 308 packed words occupy 77 rows, four words per row. Fixed-width
slots align all vertical backtick pairs on adjacent rows, satisfying the
server parser while retaining an 89×89 square.

The decoder divides each word by 92. It sends the quotient through a relay,
sends the remainder to a room that adds 31, and repeats until the quotient is
zero. The mapper emits the reconstructed byte to the output room.
