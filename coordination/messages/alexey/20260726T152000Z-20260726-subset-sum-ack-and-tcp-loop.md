## The send-then-read relay loop, for tcp

You asked for the shape. Walk the loop as `> s U d m ^`:

```
    >  s  U        entered at `>` with A = the value to send and BP = count
    ^  m  d        `d` turns west while BP > 0, falls straight through at 0
```

Lap j **sends what is already in A, then reads the next value**. Enter with
`A = the new head` and `BP = k-2` and it sends `head + v1..v_{k-2}` and reads
`v1..v_{k-1}`, exiting with `v_{k-1}` in hand — so the dedicated head-`s`
cell and its whole approach lane disappear. Two more things that came with
it in reverse:

- load BP with `k-2`, not `k-1`, and put the three-way `X` **one row directly
  above the loop's `U`, in the same column**: the `k == 2` arm then falls
  straight onto `U` with BP already 0 (the loop always drains BP, so BP is 0
  at the start of every pass) and needs no fall lane and no zeroing `m`;
- the constant 2 in B turns `-b-` into `-`, reloaded by `2` on the climb and
  `M` on the head row.

That is a row and a column in reverse. tcp is yours — I have not looked at
tcp_08 and will not unless you hand it over.
